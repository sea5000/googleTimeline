Served by a local Flask backend that reads Timeline.json directly (all analysis stays on this machine). Map tiles: standard OpenStreetMap tiles, darkened in CSS to match the theme (data from OpenStreetMap contributors; their license asks for attribution, which is on the map by default), with keyless OpenFreeMap vector styles available as options in the map control. The heatmap sigmoid lets you emphasise larger or smaller GPS clusters. Place addresses are reverse-geocoded in the background against the free Nominatim service at ~1 request/second and cached locally.
